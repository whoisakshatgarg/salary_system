Main app

Sign in once, use everywhere

The demo starts in the existing payroll app — the inventory module reuses this session, so there is no second login. Everything runs on the same offline, single-file stack as before.

2
Dashboard

One new card on the CEO dashboard

The only change to the payroll app itself: a Raw Material Inventory card. Clicking it opens the module in its own window — a browser window in dev, a second native window in the packaged .exe (that path was broken in the first build; the adversarial review caught it).

3
Stock

Every heat at a glance

The stat strip totals the whole store room — rods in stock, all-time issues, and a pro-rata ₹ stock value in Indian formatting. Each row derives remaining / received live from the usage log (never stored), with a bar and a status chip: In stock Consumed Rejected — HT-24-3302 is red because its remaining 25 rods were returned to Bharat Steels.

4
Heat detail

One heat, its whole story

HT-24-1157: purchase facts, the spectroscopy composition as chips, the mill test certificate (view / download), and the usage log — 15 rods to PO-2026-101, 10 to PO-2026-114, 35 remaining. The red button rejects the remaining batch in one click; the ✕ on any entry undoes it. Logging more rods than remain is refused, even from two windows at once.

5
New heat

Entry mirrors the mill certificate

Heat number, supplier, class / grade / shape dropdowns — each with an inline + Add new… that grows the list on the spot — plus size, rods, weight, price, and composition rows entered straight off the spectroscopy report. Half-filled rows are rejected rather than silently saved as 0%.

6
Search

Find steel by its chemistry

Composition range search: element C between 0.35% and 0.45% matches EN8 (0.42%) and EN19 (0.38%) and correctly excludes SS304 at 0.07%. Bounds are inclusive; text search, class / shape / status filters and four sort orders stack on top.

7
Usage Log

Trace any order to its steel

The global log searched for PO-2026-101: it was fed by two different heats — EN8 from HT-24-1157 and SS304 from HT-24-2231. If a customer ever reports a defect, this is the from-order-back-to-mill-certificate trail.

8
Lists

The dropdowns are yours to manage

Material classes, shapes, grades and elements are plain editable lists — add or remove freely. Removing a value never touches heats already recorded with it, and deletions stay deleted across app restarts.

Play with it yourself

The server is still running: open http://127.0.0.1:8000, sign in as admin / admin123, and click the Raw Material Inventory card on the dashboard. Try over-issuing a heat, rejecting a batch and undoing it, or adding your own grade from the form.

The four demo heats are safe to play with — say the word and they'll be wiped clean.
